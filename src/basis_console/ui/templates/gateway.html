What this view is not

Gateway Diagnostics provides operational visibility into gateway health/readiness. It does not configure the gateway, authenticate users, evaluate policy, or bypass the gateway. Future identity diagnostics (OIDC/JWKS/JWT inspection) will integrate through the future basis-identity service, not through console-owned protocol logic.
